# Changelog

## v1.1.0 — STANAG Magazines and Fika Multiplayer

- Added a 10-round 5.56x45 NATO stripper clip, sold by Peacekeeper at loyalty level 1 for $6.
- Added animated loading of detached 30- and 40-round STANAG magazines from 5.56x45 clips: Colt, HK, Magpul PMAG, TROY and FN SCAR-L. Mixed ammunition is supported.
- Added Fika 2.4.6 multiplayer support. Mosin, SKS and SVT clip reloads are visible to other players, and ammunition stays in sync between host and clients.

### Known limitation

- STANAG magazine loading is currently animated only for the player doing it. Other players do not see the animation, but the loaded ammunition is synchronised normally.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Added 5-round 7.62x54R and 10-round 7.62x39 stripper clips, sold by Prapor at loyalty level 1.
- Added animated clip reloads for the Mosin Infantry and Sniper, TOZ SKS, OP-SKS, SVT-40 and AVT-40.
- Added clip selection to the ammunition selector and optional clip preference over loose-round reloads. Native magazine swaps keep priority.
- Clip reloads require room for all rounds in the clip and a clear clip guide.
